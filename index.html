FAQ

Is my CV uploaded anywhere?

No. Parsing, scoring and drafts stay in your browser. Drafts live in local storage until you reset them.

Is this real AI?

No. This public page has no model connected; it uses a clearly labelled rule‑based analysis. Generative rewriting needs a secure backend holding an API key, which cannot safely live in a public static page.

Is the score an official ATS score?

No. It is an advisory estimate from the text found in your CV and does not predict hiring outcomes.

How do I get a PDF?

Use Download PDF in the builder and choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog.

CV writing guide

Professional summary

Two to four lines: who you are, your strongest relevant skills, what you want next.

Work experience

Lead with action verbs. Show what changed because of you, not just your duties.

Measurable achievements

Add numbers you can verify: percentages, volumes, time saved, team size.

Format choice

Reverse‑chronological, single‑column layouts are the safest for most roles.

ATS readability

Standard headings, real text rather than images, no tables for core content.

Tailoring

Mirror terms from the job post only for skills you genuinely have.

Common mistakes

Typos, vague claims, missing contact details, inconsistent dates, multi‑page padding.

Entry‑level & students

Lead with education, projects, internships, volunteering and coursework. Show initiative.
